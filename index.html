About cars

Cars are road vehicles, typically with four wheels, used for personal transportation by people rather than cargo. They use internal combustion engines or electric motors for power and are a complex technical system with subsystems for body, frame, engine, transmission, suspension, and more. Cars revolutionized transportation, impacting society profoundly, and come in many types like sedans, SUVs, and sports cars, each designed for different functions.
